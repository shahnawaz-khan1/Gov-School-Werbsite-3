About Ram Mohan Roy Seminary

Ram Mohan Roy Seminary is one of the oldest and most celebrated schools of Patna, established in 1897. The school holds a very special place in Indian history — its students took an active part in the Indian Independence Movement. Two of its students, Umakant Prasad Sinha and Ramanand Singh, made the ultimate sacrifice for the nation.

Managed under Pvt. Aided and located in the urban heart of Patna on Khajanchi Road, the school has been a beacon of quality education for over 127 years. It is a co-educational Senior Secondary school offering classes from Grade 6 to Grade 12, with Hindi as the medium of instruction.

"Ram Mohan Roy Seminary is one of the best schools in Patna. The teachers are wonderful. At one point of time it was renowned for its high standards of education — a tradition we are proud to continue into the 21st century."

— Alumni, Batch of 1999

Vision & Mission

🎯

Our Vision

To uphold the proud legacy of Ram Mohan Roy Seminary by providing quality education that builds responsible, knowledgeable and patriotic citizens — continuing a tradition of academic excellence that began in 1897.

📖

Our Mission

To provide students of Patna with a nurturing, disciplined and inclusive learning environment. We follow a State Board curriculum with CBSE-pattern instruction, ensuring every student is prepared for competitive exams and higher education with confidence.

Academic Programmes

Classes We Offer

Classes VI – VIII

Middle School

A strong foundational middle school programme focusing on core subjects — building the academic rigour and discipline needed for senior secondary success, following the State Board curriculum.

Classes IX – X

Secondary (Matric)

Our Matric programme prepares students thoroughly for Bihar Board Secondary examinations, with strong focus on Science, Mathematics, Social Studies, Hindi and English.

Classes XI – XII

Higher Secondary (+2)

The +2 Intermediate programme is offered under the State Board, following CBSE-pattern teaching. Students are prepared for competitive entrance exams and university admissions across streams.
